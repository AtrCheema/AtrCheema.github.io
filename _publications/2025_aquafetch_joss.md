AquaFetch is a free Python package that gathers over 100 separate water datasets into a single, consistent
form. Researchers who study water routinely lose days to the work it replaces, because every source
publishes its measurements in a different layout, with different units and its own quirks, before any
analysis can begin. The collection spans rainfall and river flow for approximately 48,000 river basins drawn from 36
sources worldwide, 16 water quality datasets, and over 22 thousand laboratory measurements from wastewater
treatment experiments. Ten of those were compiled here for the first time from publicly accessible online
sources. The code is deliberately structured so that others can add datasets to it, allowing the
collection to keep growing.
